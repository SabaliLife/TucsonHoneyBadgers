AAU Basketball In Tucson

How club basketball works in southern Arizona — and how Tucson kids get on the court.

What Is AAU Basketball?

AAU (Amateur Athletic Union) basketball is club basketball played outside the school season. Teams practice together year-round and compete in weekend tournaments against clubs from across Arizona and the Southwest. For young players in Tucson, AAU is where development accelerates: more reps, better competition, and coaching focused on individual growth instead of just wins.

The AAU Scene In Tucson

Tucson hoopers regularly travel the I-10 corridor for tournaments — Tucson, Phoenix, Mesa, Casa Grande — facing programs from across the state. A typical tournament weekend brings a three-game guarantee: pool play into bracket play, often four or five games if a team makes a run. That's a month of rec-league experience packed into two days.

What families should look for in a club

  • Development over trophy-chasing — practices that teach skills, not just roll the ball out.
  • A real standard — clubs that coach behavior and school habits, not just basketball. Ours is the Badger Code.
  • Honest cost structure — clear fees, payment plans, and community sponsorship. We're backed by Tucson Badger Electric.
  • Right-sized rosters — enough players to compete, few enough that everyone develops.
