Tryout FAQ

What ages can try out?

We field teams by grade level for boys and girls in the Tucson area. Tell us your player's age and grade in the form and we'll match them to the right group.

How much does it cost to play?

Club fees cover practices, tournament entries, and uniforms. We never want cost to keep a committed kid off the court - ask us about payment plans and sponsored spots backed by Tucson Badger Electric.

What should players bring to tryouts?

Basketball shoes, water, and full effort. We evaluate attitude, coachability, motor, and skill - in that order.

What if my player has never played club basketball?

Perfect. The program is development-first. Read our youth basketball guide for parents, then come compete.

When and where are tryouts held?

Tryout dates run before each season block at Tucson-area gyms. Submit the form and we'll send the next available date.
