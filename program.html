The Program

Built Tucson Tough

Skill development, real club competition, and a standard that follows players into the classroom. This is how Honey Badgers are made.

A Development-First Basketball Club

The Tucson Honey Badgers are a youth club basketball team based in Tucson, Arizona. We exist to do two things at the highest level: develop complete basketball players, and develop tough, accountable young people. One doesn't happen without the other.

Our coaches run structured, high-rep practices focused on fundamentals — shooting mechanics, ball-handling under pressure, footwork, defensive positioning, and basketball IQ. Players are pushed to compete every drill, because the Badger Code demands effort before talent.

Who the program is for

Committed young players in the Tucson area who want more than rec-league reps: real coaching, real competition on the Arizona club circuit, and a team that holds them to a standard. New to club ball? Start with our parent's guide to youth basketball in Tucson.

Inside The Hive

Year-Round Skill Training

Practices built around skill blocks: shooting, handle, finishing, defense, and live competition segments every session.

Club Tournament Play

Pool play and bracket runs across Arizona and the Southwest — see the current schedule.

Strength & Conditioning

Age-appropriate athletic development: speed, balance, body control, and injury prevention habits.

Character & Accountability

The Badger Code is coached as hard as the crossover. Grades checked, effort tracked, respect required.

The Season Structure

Spring/Summer: club tournament season — weekly practices plus tournament weekends. Fall: skills academy and scrimmage league focused on development. Winter: support for school-ball players with open gyms and individual training.
