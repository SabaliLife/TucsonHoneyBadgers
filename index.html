Guidance For Every Badger

The Badger Code

Six cells of the hive. Every player learns them, lives them, and holds their teammates to them. Read the full Code →

1

Fear Nothing

Honey badgers don't measure opponents. Play bigger than your size, every possession.

2

Outwork Everyone

Effort is a skill. First to the floor, first back on D, last to quit.

3

Protect The Hive

Team over me. We win together, we lose together, we improve together.

4

Respect All

Coaches, refs, opponents, parents. Toughness with class — that's the Badger way.

5

School First

Handle business in the classroom before the court. Grades travel with the team.

6

Bring The Energy

Bench loud, huddle locked in. Energy is contagious — be the spark, not the drain.
