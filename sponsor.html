Powered By Tucson Badger Electric

Every hive needs a power source. Ours is Tucson Badger Electric — a local, licensed electrical contractor serving Tucson and the Phoenix metro. TBE covers gym time, tournament fees, and sponsored roster spots so cost never benches a committed kid.

Same badger, same standard: show up, work relentlessly, do it right the first time.

Youth sports run on community money. When a Tucson business backs a Tucson team, fees drop, gear improves, and more kids play. It's that direct.

Back The Pack

Here's the truth about youth sports: a little bit of help from a lot of people goes a long way. You don't have to write a big check to matter to this team.

  • Funds — tournament fees, scholarships, uniforms.
  • Gear — balls, shoes, practice equipment.
  • Gym time — courts and practice space.
  • Travel & meals — getting the pack to tournaments, fed.
  • Time & skills — photography, scorekeeping, carpools, first aid, anything.

Every contribution gets acknowledged. Business sponsors get a spot on the wall above.
